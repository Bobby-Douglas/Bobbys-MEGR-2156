a.	Primary Product Function:The primary function of an Owala Freesip water bottle is to contain and seal a liquid while minimizing the heat transfer between the liquid and the surrounding environment. Mechanically, the water bottle uses a sealable containment system to avoid leakage while using an insulated wall structure to reduce the rate of thermal energy transfer out or into the liquid. Using multiple layers of insulation, it includes a vacuum-insulated region between the walls to reduce heat transfer by conduction and convection. As the Vacuum contains very little matter within, very few particles are able to transfer thermal energy away. 

b.	Governing Model: As the primary use of the Owala water bottle is to reduce energy transfer it’s governing equation is 

Q˙=ΔT/Rtotal

 which describes the rate of heat transfer between a liquid and the external world. Q is the rate of heat transfer, ΔT is the temperature difference between the liquid and the external environment, and Rtotal is the total thermal resistance of the Owala’s insulation.The vacuum between the inner and outer wall greatly decreases the rate of heat transfer through conduction and convection.

The assumption that makes this model valid for the product is that the temperature externally and internally remains the same during the equation. In reality, outside and inside temperatures are fluctuating constantly

Glass tumbler with silicone lid: using a linear-actuated silicone gasket enclosure that uses compressive elastomeric force and static friction to maintain a watertight seal against a single-wall glass container.

ii. Design Decision

One noticeable design decision was the use of silicone to create the seal on the top of the water bottle instead of rubber or just plastic on plastic. The silicone extends around the rim of the opening portion of the lid to create a seamless contact point around the edge of the water bottle. This allows for full contact when a compressive force is applied by the locking mechanism to create the seal. I believe silicone was chosen over other items like rubber due to its low compression requirements, reducing the strain on the other plastic components. Silicone also has a naturally high Microscopic conformability, or ability to deform precisely to adapt to other surfaces, further reducing the necessary compressive force, prolonging product lifespans.
